# Synchronized teaching steps

Approved design: spoken phrases and board actions form explicit ordered steps. Each step owns an immutable recording. Use measured recording duration, not character-proportional speech estimates. Preserve Neon/Inngest checkpoints, old lesson references, local SQLite, and the separate hero branch. No new paid service or Python worker.

Split a scene at exact, ordered writing anchors; keep narration verbatim. Unanchored explanations and audience questions become speech-only steps. A question cannot share a result-writing step. Missing or out-of-order anchors fail validation rather than inventing timing. Decorations belong to the operation they decorate. Remove mandatory blank-board opening, title/underline ritual, six-scene minimum, forced quiz and blanket reading holds for newly generated lessons.

Persist individual step WAVs and checkpoint their references. Assemble a canonical scene WAV for existing recovery semantics. After reviews and mathematical checks, map final board actions to recorded steps and compile each step with its measured speech duration. Cache identities include a step-recording version. Do not overwrite existing recordings. Playback waits for audio rather than silently racing ahead. A bounded natural speech-rate adjustment may lengthen a short recording to fit readable ink; reject a pairing requiring unnatural speech instead of rushing handwriting or adding long silent tails.

Acceptance: x+2=5 starts on the equation, introduces subtraction before revealing 3, never displays an answer during an audience question, maintains readable ink, and advances within a brief transition after speech/writing. Seeking and playback-speed changes share the same timeline. Tests cover malformed anchors, question order, WAV validation, cached recovery, missing audio, no automatic title underline, no estimated-duration tail, and immutable legacy playback. Deploy only to the generation-test project after tests/build/review; listening remains a user review requirement.
